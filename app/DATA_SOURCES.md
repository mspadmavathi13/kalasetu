# Data sources and scope

1. Coder-Dragon, Traditional and Handmade Indian Jewellery Dataset
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/Coder-Dragon/indian-traditional-artificial-jewellery
   License: Apache-2.0
   6,953 rows reported by the dataset viewer.
   Fields include product name, description, features, price, brand, product type, quantity and dimensions.

2. Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
   https://www.craftclustersofindia.in/site/home.aspx
   The portal reports 35,312 products across 32 craft categories and provides the broader Indian handicraft taxonomy.

3. Ranganathan, Aruna — The Artisan and His Audience
   Stanford / Administrative Science Quarterly.
   A field experiment collected 455 Channapatna bangle sales transactions. This source is useful as contextual evidence about artisan pricing behavior, but the public material located for this build does not provide a complete transaction-level CSV, so it is NOT used as fabricated training rows.
